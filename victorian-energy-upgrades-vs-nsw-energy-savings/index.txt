<h2 id="direct-answer-what-is-the-difference-between-veu-and-ess">Direct Answer: What is the difference between VEU and ESS?</h2>
<p>Victorian Energy Upgrades is Victoria’s energy-efficiency upgrade program, while the Energy Savings Scheme is New South Wales’ energy-saving scheme. Victorian households and businesses should check VEU-related upgrade options, while NSW households and businesses should check ESS-related pathways where applicable. You should not apply VEU rules to NSW or ESS rules to Victoria.</p>
<h2 id="1-why-this-confusion-matters">1. Why this confusion matters</h2>
<p>Confusing VEU and ESS can lead to wrong expectations. A homeowner in Victoria might see a claim about NSW and assume it applies to them. A NSW business might read about a Victorian upgrade and expect the same product, discount or process. This can create disappointment, delays and poor decision-making.</p>

<h2 id="2-what-is-victorian-energy-upgrades">2. What is Victorian Energy Upgrades?</h2>
<p>Victorian Energy Upgrades is a Victorian energy-efficiency program that helps eligible households and businesses access discounts on approved energy-efficient products and services. It is commonly connected with upgrades such as hot water systems, heating and cooling, and other approved energy-saving products.</p>

<h2 id="3-what-is-the-nsw-energy-savings-scheme">3. What is the NSW Energy Savings Scheme?</h2>
<p>The NSW Energy Savings Scheme supports energy-saving activities in New South Wales. It encourages eligible energy-efficiency upgrades by creating incentives for approved activities, products and providers.</p>
<p>For NSW customers, the key point is that ESS is not the same as VEU. The name, state, rules and process are different. A NSW household or business should not rely on Victorian upgrade claims when deciding whether to replace a hot water system, improve energy efficiency or request a quote.</p>
<p>Good questions for NSW customers include:</p>
<ul><li>Is my property in New South Wales?</li>
