<h2 id="direct-answer-how-does-filtered-water-reach-every-tap">Direct Answer: How does filtered water reach every tap?</h2>
<p>A whole home filtration system is installed on the main water supply entering the property. Water passes through the filtration stages before moving through the household plumbing.</p>
<p>As a result, filtered water can be supplied to connected areas such as:</p>
<ul><li>kitchen taps</li>
<li>bathroom basins</li>
<li>showers</li>
<li>baths</li>
<li>laundry taps</li>
<li>toilets</li>
<li>dishwashers</li>
<li>washing machines</li>
<li>other connected water outlets</li>
</ul>
<p>The exact installation arrangement depends on the property’s plumbing layout. A suitability assessment is required before confirming where the system can be installed and which water lines it will serve.</p>
<h2 id="what-does-filtration-at-the-source-mean">What does “filtration at the source” mean?</h2>
<p>Filtration at the source means treating the household water supply before it is divided between different rooms and outlets.</p>
<p>An under-sink filter is normally connected to one selected kitchen tap. A whole home filtration system is positioned earlier in the plumbing network so the water is filtered before travelling throughout the property.</p>
<p>This does not mean every filtration system is suitable for every home. Installation space, plumbing access, water pressure, servicing access and household requirements should all be reviewed first.</p>

<h2 id="stage-1-sediment-filtration">Stage 1: Sediment filtration</h2>
<p>The first stage is designed to target sediment and particles that may be present in the water supply or household plumbing.</p>
<p>The brochure identifies materials such as:</p>
<ul><li>dust</li>
<li>dirt</li>
<li>rust</li>
<li>sand</li>
<li>silt</li>
<li>sediment</li>
<li>algae</li>
</ul>
<p>Reducing these particles at the entry point means the water has already passed through sediment filtration before reaching connected household taps.</p>
<p>This stage should be described as being designed to reduce sediment. It should not be used to make unsupported claims about local water quality or to suggest that Derrimut’s water supply is unsafe.</p>
<h2 id="stage-2-mineral-particle-reduction">Stage 2: Mineral-particle reduction</h2>
<p>The second stage is designed to target mineral particles, including particles associated with calcium and magnesium.</p>
<p>This stage may help limit scale build-up on household fixtures and water-using appliances. Scale may become noticeable around taps, shower fittings or other areas where water is frequently used.</p>
<p>The potential result will depend on the property, incoming water, household usage and ongoing system maintenance. Whole-home filtration should not be presented as guaranteeing a scale-free home.</p>
<h2 id="stage-3-chlorine-taste-and-odour-improvement">Stage 3: Chlorine, taste and odour improvement</h2>
<p>The third stage is designed to help improve chlorine-related taste and odour and address other brochure-listed impurities.</p>
<p>Because this filtration occurs before water is distributed through the household, the filtered supply can reach more than one selected drinking-water tap.</p>
<p>This may be relevant for households that want filtration across:</p>
<ul><li>drinking and cooking areas</li>
<li>bathroom basins and showers</li>
<li>laundry taps</li>
<li>connected water-using appliances</li>
</ul>
<p>The system should not be promoted as treating disease, preventing illness or providing guaranteed health outcomes.</p>
<h2 id="how-filtration-may-support-plumbing-and-appliances">How filtration may support plumbing and appliances</h2>
<p>Whole home water filtration is also positioned around protecting plumbing and water-using appliances from sediment and mineral particles.</p>
<p>By filtering the supply before it moves through household pipes, the system is designed to reduce the amount of selected material reaching connected fixtures and appliances.</p>
<p>This may be relevant to:</p>
<ul><li>tapware</li>
<li>shower fittings</li>
<li>washing machines</li>
<li>dishwashers</li>
<li>hot water systems</li>
<li>household plumbing</li>
</ul>
<p>The extent of any benefit will vary. It depends on the property, water conditions, usage, appliance design and filtration maintenance.</p>
<p>Auzbright can explain the product’s intended functions, but homeowners should avoid expecting identical results across every property.</p>
