Whole Home Water Filtration in Derrimut: Filtered Water at Every Tap

7 August 20266 min readBy the Auzbright team

Most household water filters are associated with one drinking-water tap in the kitchen. Whole home water filtration works differently. It filters water closer to the point where the supply enters the property, before that water is distributed to individual taps, showers and water-using appliances.

If you are considering filtered water throughout your Derrimut property, Auzbright can explain how the system works and assess whether whole-home filtration suits your household and installation conditions.

Direct Answer: How does filtered water reach every tap?

A whole home filtration system is installed on the main water supply entering the property. Water passes through the filtration stages before moving through the household plumbing.

As a result, filtered water can be supplied to connected areas such as:

  • kitchen taps
  • bathroom basins
  • showers
  • baths
  • laundry taps
  • toilets
  • dishwashers
  • washing machines
  • other connected water outlets

The exact installation arrangement depends on the property’s plumbing layout. A suitability assessment is required before confirming where the system can be installed and which water lines it will serve.

What does “filtration at the source” mean?

Filtration at the source means treating the household water supply before it is divided between different rooms and outlets.

An under-sink filter is normally connected to one selected kitchen tap. A whole home filtration system is positioned earlier in the plumbing network so the water is filtered before travelling throughout the property.

This does not mean every filtration system is suitable for every home. Installation space, plumbing access, water pressure, servicing access and household requirements should all be reviewed first.

How Auzbright’s three-stage filtration system works

Auzbright’s Whole Home Water Filtration System uses three filtration stages. Each stage has a different purpose within the overall filtration process.

Filtration StagePrimary Purpose
Stage 1: Sediment filtrationDesigned to reduce sediment and visible particles such as dirt, rust, sand and silt
Stage 2: Mineral-particle reductionHelps reduce mineral particles and may help limit scale build-up
Stage 3: Chlorine, taste and odour improvementHelps improve chlorine-related taste and odour before water travels through the home

Stage 1: Sediment filtration

The first stage is designed to target sediment and particles that may be present in the water supply or household plumbing.

The brochure identifies materials such as:

  • dust
  • dirt
  • rust
  • sand
  • silt
  • sediment
  • algae

Reducing these particles at the entry point means the water has already passed through sediment filtration before reaching connected household taps.

This stage should be described as being designed to reduce sediment. It should not be used to make unsupported claims about local water quality or to suggest that Derrimut’s water supply is unsafe.

Stage 2: Mineral-particle reduction

The second stage is designed to target mineral particles, including particles associated with calcium and magnesium.

This stage may help limit scale build-up on household fixtures and water-using appliances. Scale may become noticeable around taps, shower fittings or other areas where water is frequently used.

The potential result will depend on the property, incoming water, household usage and ongoing system maintenance. Whole-home filtration should not be presented as guaranteeing a scale-free home.

Stage 3: Chlorine, taste and odour improvement

The third stage is designed to help improve chlorine-related taste and odour and address other brochure-listed impurities.

Because this filtration occurs before water is distributed through the household, the filtered supply can reach more than one selected drinking-water tap.

This may be relevant for households that want filtration across:

  • drinking and cooking areas
  • bathroom basins and showers
  • laundry taps
  • connected water-using appliances

The system should not be promoted as treating disease, preventing illness or providing guaranteed health outcomes.

How filtration may support plumbing and appliances

Whole home water filtration is also positioned around protecting plumbing and water-using appliances from sediment and mineral particles.

By filtering the supply before it moves through household pipes, the system is designed to reduce the amount of selected material reaching connected fixtures and appliances.

This may be relevant to:

  • tapware
  • shower fittings
  • washing machines
  • dishwashers
  • hot water systems
  • household plumbing

The extent of any benefit will vary. It depends on the property, water conditions, usage, appliance design and filtration maintenance.

Auzbright can explain the product’s intended functions, but homeowners should avoid expecting identical results across every property.

What should Derrimut homeowners check before installation?

Before installing a whole home water filtration system, homeowners should ask several practical questions.

Is there enough installation space?

The system needs a suitable location near the incoming water supply. The installer should also have safe access for installation and future servicing.

Can the main water line be accessed?

The existing plumbing layout affects where filtration can be installed and whether additional plumbing work may be needed.

Which taps and appliances will receive filtered water?

The quote should explain how the system will connect to the property and which household water lines it is expected to serve.

How will servicing be completed?

Filtration cartridges need maintenance. Requirements depend on incoming water quality, household usage and the selected cartridge system.

Is the system suitable for the property?

Household size, water use, plumbing arrangement and available space should be reviewed before installation is confirmed.

What is the purpose of the built-in bypass?

The brochure refers to a built-in bypass as part of the system. A bypass can support practical system management by allowing the filtration section to be isolated when appropriate, such as during certain servicing situations.

The installer should explain how the bypass works, when it may be used and what the homeowner should or should not operate.

Homeowners should not make changes to the system without appropriate guidance.

Whole home filtration is not the same as a VEU upgrade

Whole Home Water Filtration must be considered separately from Victorian Energy Upgrades.

Auzbright provides several energy-efficiency and home-upgrade services, but this does not mean every service is connected to a government incentive. No VEU eligibility, rebate or government discount should be assumed for water filtration unless official confirmation is provided.

The filtration decision should instead be based on:

  • household filtration goals
  • property suitability
  • plumbing conditions
  • installation requirements
  • system features
  • maintenance needs
  • a property-specific quote

FAQs

Does Auzbright install whole home water filtration in Derrimut?

Auzbright is based in Derrimut and offers a Whole Home Water Filtration System. Property suitability, installation scope and service availability should be confirmed through an enquiry.

Will whole home filtration supply filtered water to every tap?

The system is designed to filter water before it is distributed through the property. The taps and appliances served will depend on the home’s plumbing layout and installation design.

The system includes sediment filtration, mineral-particle reduction, and chlorine, taste and odour improvement. Each stage performs a different role in the filtration process.
