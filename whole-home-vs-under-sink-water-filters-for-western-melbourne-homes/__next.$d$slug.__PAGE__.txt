<p>Auzbright is based in Derrimut and offers a three-stage Whole Home Water Filtration System for suitable properties. If you are comparing whole-home and under-sink filtration in western Melbourne, the first step is to decide whether you want filtration at one drinking-water point or throughout the property.</p>
<h2 id="direct-answer-which-filtration-system-should-you-choose">Direct Answer: Which filtration system should you choose?</h2>
<p>Choose an under-sink system when your main goal is filtered water from one selected kitchen tap. Consider whole home filtration when you want water filtered before it reaches multiple connected household outlets.</p>
<p>Whole-home filtration involves a broader installation and servicing scope, while under-sink filtration is usually more targeted. Property suitability should be assessed before either option is selected.</p>
<h2 id="1-start-with-the-water-outlets-you-want-to-filter">1. Start with the water outlets you want to filter</h2>
<p>The clearest difference between the two systems is coverage.</p>
<p>An under-sink filter is normally installed beneath the kitchen sink and connected to a dedicated or selected tap. Its purpose is generally to provide filtered water for drinking, cooking and filling containers.</p>
<p>Whole home filtration works earlier in the plumbing system. It filters water near the point where the supply enters the property, before that water travels to connected outlets.</p>
<p>This may include:</p>
<ul><li>kitchen taps</li>
<li>bathroom basins</li>
<li>showers and baths</li>
<li>laundry taps</li>
<li>washing machines</li>
<li>dishwashers</li>
<li>hot water systems</li>
<li>other connected fixtures</li>
</ul>
<p>The exact outlets served depend on the home’s plumbing layout and installation design.</p>

<h2 id="3-how-whole-home-filtration-works">3. How whole home filtration works</h2>
<p>A whole home system is connected to the main incoming water line. Water passes through the filtration stages before moving through the property’s internal plumbing.</p>
<p>Auzbright’s system uses three stages:</p>
<h3><b>Stage 1: Sediment filtration</b></h3>
<p>The first stage is designed to reduce sediment and particles such as dirt, dust, rust, sand and silt.</p>
<h3><b>Stage 2: Mineral-particle reduction</b></h3>
<p>The second stage is designed to reduce mineral particles and may help limit scale build-up around taps, fixtures and water-using appliances.</p>
<h3><b>Stage 3: Chlorine, taste and odour improvement</b></h3>
<p>The third stage helps improve chlorine-related taste and odour before water reaches connected household outlets.</p>
<p>Results and maintenance needs vary according to the property, incoming water conditions and household usage.</p>
<h2 id="4-how-under-sink-filtration-works">4. How under-sink filtration works</h2>
<p>An under-sink system is installed beneath a kitchen sink and connected to one selected outlet.</p>
<p>This can suit households that mainly want filtered water for:</p>
<ul><li>drinking</li>
<li>cooking</li>
<li>preparing tea or coffee</li>
<li>filling bottles</li>
<li>washing fruit and vegetables</li>
</ul>
<p>Because it focuses on one point, it does not normally filter the water used in showers, laundries or other rooms unless separate systems are installed.</p>
<p>The filter type, cartridge requirements and intended performance depend on the selected product. These details should be checked through the product documentation rather than assumed.</p>
<h2 id="5-compare-installation-requirements">5. Compare installation requirements</h2>
<p>Whole home filtration usually requires more installation planning because it connects to the property’s main water supply.</p>
<p>The assessment may need to review:</p>
<ul><li>where the water line enters the property</li>
<li>available space for the filtration system</li>
<li>access to existing plumbing</li>
<li>safe servicing access</li>
<li>the location of the filtration cabinet</li>
<li>whether plumbing changes are required</li>
<li>whether a built-in bypass is included and how it operates</li>
</ul>
<p>Under-sink installation is more localised, but it still needs suitable cupboard space, plumbing access and room for cartridge replacement.</p>
<p>For apartments, townhouses or properties with shared infrastructure, approval or common-property considerations may also need to be checked.</p>
<h2 id="6-consider-servicing-and-cartridge-access">6. Consider servicing and cartridge access</h2>
<p>Both filtration systems require maintenance. The difference is where and how that maintenance occurs.</p>
<p>With under-sink filtration, cartridges are normally accessed inside the kitchen cabinet. Storage items may need to be moved when servicing is required.</p>
<p>With whole home filtration, the system should be installed where the cartridges and bypass can be reached safely without obstructing daily use.</p>
<p>Maintenance requirements depend on:</p>
<ul><li>incoming water quality</li>
<li>household consumption</li>
<li>filter design</li>
<li>cartridge type</li>
<li>manufacturer guidance</li>
<li>servicing conditions</li>
</ul>
<p>Homeowners should avoid relying on a universal cartridge replacement schedule unless it is supported by the supplied product documentation.</p>
<h2 id="7-think-about-plumbing-and-appliance-protection">7. Think about plumbing and appliance protection</h2>
<p>One reason homeowners consider whole home filtration is that water is filtered before it travels through household plumbing.</p>
<p>By reducing selected sediment and mineral particles at the entry point, the system may help limit the amount of this material reaching:</p>
<ul><li>tapware</li>
<li>shower fittings</li>
<li>washing machines</li>
<li>dishwashers</li>
<li>hot water systems</li>
<li>internal plumbing</li>
</ul>
<p>An under-sink filter does not normally provide this broader coverage because it treats water after it has already travelled through most of the property.</p>
<p>These potential benefits should remain conditional. Whole home filtration should not be described as guaranteeing scale-free plumbing or preventing every appliance problem.</p>
<h2 id="8-could-a-household-use-both-systems">8. Could a household use both systems?</h2>
<p>Some households may choose whole home filtration for water throughout the property and a separate under-sink system for a more specific drinking-water requirement.</p>
<p>A combined approach may be considered when the household wants:</p>
<ul><li>broader sediment and mineral-particle reduction</li>
<li>filtration for bathrooms and laundries</li>
<li>an additional drinking-water stage at the kitchen</li>
<li>different filtration goals for different outlets</li>
</ul>
<p>Whether both systems are necessary depends on household preferences, the whole-home product selected and the intended use of the under-sink filter.</p>
<p>A provider should explain the role of each system clearly so the household does not pay for duplicated functions it does not need.</p>
<h2 id="9-which-option-is-better-during-a-renovation">9. Which option is better during a renovation?</h2>
<p>Renovation can be a useful time to consider water filtration because plumbing access and equipment locations may be easier to plan before walls, cabinets and landscaping are completed.</p>
<p>For whole home filtration, early planning can help determine:</p>
<ul><li>the main water entry point</li>
<li>cabinet location</li>
<li>pipe routing</li>
<li>servicing clearance</li>
<li>outdoor access</li>
<li>protection from weather</li>
<li>coordination with hot water equipment</li>
</ul>
<p>For under-sink filtration, homeowners can plan cabinet space, tap placement and plumbing connections before the kitchen is finished.</p>
<p>Auzbright can discuss whole-home filtration during the planning stage so the proposed location can be reviewed before other renovation decisions are finalised.</p>
<h2 id="10-compare-the-full-quote">10. Compare the full quote</h2>
<p>Do not compare filtration systems by equipment price alone.</p>
<p>A quote should explain:</p>
<ul><li>the filtration system supplied</li>
<li>number and purpose of filtration stages</li>
<li>installation work</li>
<li>plumbing changes</li>
<li>bypass arrangement where applicable</li>
<li>cartridge access</li>
<li>maintenance expectations</li>
<li>exclusions</li>
<li>possible site-specific costs</li>
<li>product support</li>
<li>any warranty terms supported by documentation</li>
</ul>
<p>The homeowner should also understand which taps and appliances the system is expected to serve.</p>
