24:T2769,<p>If your old air conditioner is struggling to cool or heat your home properly, making more noise than usual, or becoming expensive to run, it may be time to review your upgrade options. For many Victorian households, a split system air conditioner can be a practical way to improve comfort while choosing a more energy-efficient heating and cooling solution.</p>
<p>A split system air conditioner is commonly used for individual rooms, living areas, bedrooms, home offices, and open-plan spaces. But before replacing an old unit, homeowners should check more than just the unit price. The right upgrade depends on your current system, room size, property layout, installation conditions, product suitability, and eligibility under current Victorian requirements.</p>
<p>If you are considering a more efficient heating and cooling system, Auzbright can help you understand your air conditioning upgrade options and eligibility before you decide.</p>
<h2 id="direct-answer-what-should-you-check-before-replacing-an-old-">Direct Answer: What should you check before replacing an old split system in Victoria?</h2>
<p>Before replacing an old split system air conditioner in Victoria, check your existing unit, room size, heating and cooling needs, installation location, electrical setup, product eligibility, quote details, and whether your property may qualify for available support under the Victorian Energy Upgrades program. Eligibility and suitability can vary, so it is best to request an assessment before approving the upgrade.</p>
<h2 id="1-start-with-the-problem-you-are-trying-to-solve">1. Start with the problem you are trying to solve</h2>
<p>Not every air conditioning upgrade starts for the same reason. Some homeowners want better cooling in summer. Others need more reliable heating in winter. Some are dealing with an older unit that still runs but no longer performs well.</p>
<p>Common signs your old air conditioner may need attention include:</p>
<ul><li>weak cooling or heating</li>
<li>rising running costs</li>
<li>frequent repairs</li>
<li>unusual noise</li>
<li>poor airflow</li>
<li>uneven comfort across the room</li>
<li>an older system that may not be efficient</li>
<li>a unit that no longer suits how the room is used</li>
</ul>
<p>Understanding the main problem helps avoid choosing a replacement that looks suitable on paper but does not match your actual comfort needs.</p>
<h2 id="2-check-whether-a-split-system-is-the-right-type-of-upgrade">2. Check whether a split system is the right type of upgrade</h2>
<p>A split system can be a good option for targeted heating and cooling. It is often used when a homeowner wants to improve comfort in a specific space rather than condition the whole house through ducted air conditioning.</p>
<p>A split system may suit:</p>
<h4>Home Situation</h4>
<h4>Why a Split System May Be Considered</h4>
<p>Living room or bedroom upgrade</p>
<p>Helps manage comfort in a frequently used space</p>
<p>Living room or bedroom upgrade</p>
<p>Helps manage comfort in a frequently used space</p>
<p>Older single-room unit replacement</p>
<p>Can replace a unit that is inefficient or unreliable</p>
<p>Targeted heating and cooling</p>
<p>Useful when whole-home ducted air conditioning is not required</p>
<p>However, split systems are not automatically the best choice for every home. Some properties may need multiple units, while others may be better suited to ducted or multi-split options. Auzbright can help homeowners compare upgrade options based on property layout and comfort needs.</p>
<h2 id="3-review-room-size-and-usage">3. Review room size and usage</h2>
<p>The size and use of the room matter. A system that is too small may struggle to heat or cool effectively. A system that is too large may not be the most suitable choice either. Factors such as ceiling height, insulation, window size, sun exposure, open-plan layout, and how often the room is used can all affect the recommendation.</p>
<p>Before requesting a quote, consider:</p>
<ul><li>Which room needs the upgrade?</li>
<li>Is it open-plan or enclosed?</li>
<li>Is the room used mainly during the day or at night?</li>
<li>Does the space get strong afternoon sun?</li>
<li>Are there large windows or poor insulation?</li>
<li>Do you need heating, cooling, or both?</li>
</ul>
<p>These details help guide a more practical recommendation.</p>
<h2 id="4-understand-the-victorian-energy-upgrades-connection">4. Understand the Victorian Energy Upgrades connection</h2>
<p>In Victoria, eligible heating and cooling upgrades may be supported through the Victorian Energy Upgrades program. This can help households access discounts on approved energy-efficient products when the upgrade meets current program requirements.</p>
<p>For split system air conditioning, homeowners should avoid assuming that every property, product, or installation automatically qualifies. Eligibility can depend on the existing system, the approved replacement product, installation conditions, property details, and current program rules.</p>
<p>Auzbright helps Victorian homeowners understand whether their air conditioning upgrade may be eligible and what information is needed before moving ahead.</p>
<h2 id="5-ask-what-is-included-in-the-quote">5. Ask what is included in the quote</h2>
<p>A clear quote should explain more than the product name. It should help you understand what is included, what may cost extra, and what needs to happen before installation.</p>
<p>Ask about:</p>
<ul><li>supply and installation</li>
<li>removal or decommissioning of the old unit if required</li>
<li>electrical work</li>
<li>wall bracket or outdoor unit placement</li>
<li>pipework and drainage</li>
<li>certificates or paperwork</li>
<li>eligibility assessment</li>
<li>any site-specific requirements</li>
</ul>
<p>Do not rely only on a headline offer. A proper quote should match your property, not just advertise a generic upgrade.</p>
<h2 id="6-check-installation-location-before-choosing-a-unit">6. Check installation location before choosing a unit</h2>
<p>The indoor and outdoor unit locations both matter. The indoor unit should suit the room layout and airflow needs. The outdoor unit needs safe access, suitable ventilation, and a practical position that does not create unnecessary noise or access issues.</p>
<p>Installation planning may be affected by:</p>
<ul><li>wall type</li>
<li>distance between indoor and outdoor units</li>
<li>drainage path</li>
<li>electrical access</li>
<li>outdoor space</li>
<li>body corporate or rental property requirements</li>
<li>safe working access</li>
</ul>
<p>This is why a property-specific review is important before confirming an upgrade.</p>
<h2 id="7-be-careful-with-savings-and-rebate-claims">7. Be careful with savings and rebate claims</h2>
<p>A more efficient air conditioning system may help reduce energy use compared with an older or less efficient unit, but results vary. Actual savings can depend on system size, usage habits, temperature settings, insulation, weather, tariffs, and installation quality.</p>
<p>The same applies to rebates or discounts. Upgrade support may be available for eligible properties and approved products, but it should not be presented as guaranteed for every home.</p>
<p>Safe questions to ask include:</p>
<ul><li>Is my current system eligible for replacement?</li>
<li>Is the proposed model approved under current requirements?</li>
<li>What discount or support may apply to my situation?</li>
<li>Are there any conditions I should understand first?</li>
<li>What will I need to provide for the eligibility check?</li>
</ul>
<p>Auzbright can guide you through these questions before you choose your upgrade.</p>
