Split air conditioner rebate

Cool savings await: split air conditioner rebates with a government program.

Upgrade your comfort and save with government rebates of up to $9,000.

Apply now

Get your application started for the split system air conditioner government program

  • Start your application for the split system air conditioner government program. Confirm your eligibility, and we’ll reach out with all the necessary details.
  • Under a government initiative in Victoria, homeowners can take advantage of rebates of up to $9,000 to help upgrade split system air conditioning units.
  • This incentive aims to encourage the adoption of energy-efficient and environmentally friendly cooling solutions in households, helping reduce energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Join us in building a more sustainable and energy-conscious future for Victoria.
Getting started

How easy is it?

Take advantage of the Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program and receive free exhaust fan seals for every residence in Victoria. These self-closing fan seals enhance weatherproofing, boosting energy efficiency and lowering energy costs. Certified A-grade electricians handle the installation for hassle-free service.
