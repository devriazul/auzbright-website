Free upgrade process

How does it work?

  1. 01

    Draws surrounding air

    A fan pulls air containing heat energy across the evaporator.

  2. 02

    Turns air into gas

    The evaporator transforms refrigerant into a gas, compressed into a hot gas.

  3. 03

    Heats water with gas

    Hot gas in the condenser loop warms water inside the tank coil.

  4. 04

    Produces hot water

    Refrigerant returns to a fluid after heating the water, ready for the cycle to repeat.

Uses up to 70% less energy

Benefits of heat pumps

  • Saves money in the long term.
  • Energy-efficient and environmentally friendly.
  • Fewer greenhouse gas emissions than conventional systems.
  • Effective in lower temperatures.
  • No need for additional solar panels or batteries.
  • Easy installation and connection to your hot water plumbing.
Why upgrade

Why upgrade to a heat pump hot water system

Ensure your hot water system is regularly maintained to save on energy costs. Heat pump systems offer excellent value for businesses seeking to maximise solar electricity usage. They use heat from the air to heat water efficiently, reducing reliance on grid power.
