<h2 id="direct-answer-can-derrimut-households-access-veu-support">Direct Answer: Can Derrimut households access VEU support?</h2>
<p>Yes, some Derrimut households may be able to access VEU support for eligible heat pump hot water and reverse-cycle air-conditioning upgrades.</p>
<p>Derrimut is in Victoria, so properties in the area may fall within the geographic scope of the Victorian program. However, location is only one part of the assessment. The existing system, replacement product, installation conditions and current activity rules must also qualify.</p>
<p>Auzbright can help homeowners understand which information is needed before a quote or eligibility outcome can be confirmed.</p>
<h2 id="what-is-the-victorian-energy-upgrades-program">What is the Victorian Energy Upgrades program?</h2>
<p>Victorian Energy Upgrades is a state energy-efficiency program that supports qualifying homes and businesses in upgrading to approved energy-efficient products and services.</p>
<p>Discounts are funded through Victorian Energy Efficiency Certificates created when eligible upgrade activities are completed under the program.</p>
<p>Auzbright is not the Victorian Government. Its role is to help customers understand available upgrade options, collect the required property and system information, and guide them through the relevant assessment process where applicable.</p>
<h2 id="which-hot-water-upgrades-may-be-relevant">Which hot water upgrades may be relevant?</h2>
<p>Heat pump hot water is one of the upgrade categories currently associated with VEU support.</p>
<p>A heat pump system uses electricity to transfer heat from the surrounding air into stored water. It may be considered when replacing certain existing hot water systems, but eligibility should never be assumed from the property address alone.</p>
<p>The assessment may consider:</p>
<ul><li>whether the current system is gas, electric or solar boosted</li>
<li>whether the existing system is functioning</li>
<li>the type of replacement proposed</li>
<li>whether the replacement product meets current requirements</li>
<li>property access and installation conditions</li>
<li>household hot water demand</li>
<li>plumbing and electrical requirements</li>
<li>the documentation needed for the upgrade</li>
</ul>
<p>Only products that meet current program requirements should be proposed through a VEU pathway.</p>
<h2 id="which-air-conditioning-upgrades-may-be-relevant">Which air-conditioning upgrades may be relevant?</h2>
<p>Efficient reverse-cycle air conditioners can provide both heating and cooling. Depending on the current system and program rules, eligible upgrades may include suitable single-split, multi-split or ducted reverse-cycle options.</p>
<p>The existing heating system is important. A household replacing one non-ducted gas room heater may follow a different pathway from a property replacing ducted gas heating or another existing system.</p>
<p>An air-conditioning eligibility review may consider:</p>
<ul><li>the type of heater already installed</li>
<li>whether it is ducted or non-ducted</li>
<li>which rooms or zones need heating and cooling</li>
<li>the proposed reverse-cycle system</li>
<li>product eligibility</li>
<li>system capacity and design</li>
<li>electrical readiness</li>
<li>indoor and outdoor unit placement</li>
<li>installation and decommissioning requirements</li>
</ul>
<p>The same support level or installation arrangement should not be assumed for every Derrimut home.</p>

<h2 id="does-every-derrimut-property-qualify">Does every Derrimut property qualify?</h2>
<p>No. A Derrimut address does not guarantee VEU eligibility.</p>
<p>A property may not qualify if:</p>
<ul><li>the existing system does not match the relevant activity</li>
<li>the proposed product is not eligible</li>
<li>documentation cannot confirm the current system</li>
<li>installation requirements cannot be met</li>
<li>the upgrade has already been completed</li>
<li>required approvals are missing</li>
<li>current program rules have changed</li>
<li>the customer or property does not meet the applicable conditions</li>
</ul>
<p>A responsible provider should explain that eligibility is conditional rather than promising approval before the assessment is complete.</p>
<h2 id="can-landlords-and-rental-properties-apply">Can landlords and rental properties apply?</h2>
<p>Rental properties may be considered where the property, existing system and proposed upgrade meet current requirements. However, the owner generally needs to approve permanent installation work.</p>
<p>Landlords and property managers should also consider:</p>
<ul><li>tenant access</li>
<li>installation timing</li>
<li>owners-corporation requirements</li>
<li>responsibility for approvals</li>
<li>old-system removal</li>
<li>compliance paperwork</li>
<li>future maintenance</li>
</ul>
<p>Tenants should not assume they can approve a permanent upgrade without the property owner’s permission.</p>
<h2 id="what-can-change-the-final-out-of-pocket-cost">What can change the final out-of-pocket cost?</h2>
<p>Even when an upgrade may qualify for VEU support, the final amount paid by the household can vary.</p>
<p>Factors may include:</p>
<ul><li>the selected product</li>
<li>the existing system</li>
<li>installation complexity</li>
<li>additional electrical or plumbing work</li>
<li>removal or decommissioning</li>
<li>access limitations</li>
<li>system size</li>
<li>zoning or duct requirements</li>
<li>required co-payment</li>
<li>current certificate and program settings</li>
</ul>
<p>This is why the eligibility outcome and the installation quote should be considered together.</p>
<h2 id="questions-to-ask-before-accepting-an-offer">Questions to ask before accepting an offer</h2>
<p>Before booking a hot water or air-conditioning upgrade, ask:</p>
<ol><li>Which VEU activity is being used?</li>

<li>Is the proposed product currently approved?</li>
<li>What evidence is required?</li>
<li>What is included in the quote?</li>
<li>What work may cost extra?</li>

<li>What happens to the old system?</li>
<li>What certificates or documents will I receive?</li>
<li>Could program rules change before installation?</li>
</ol>
<p>Clear answers help homeowners understand the actual upgrade rather than relying only on the headline offer.</p>
