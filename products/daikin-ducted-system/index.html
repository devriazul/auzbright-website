Overview

About the Daikin ducted system

Daikin ducted air conditioning combines a concealed indoor unit, a zone controller and a compact outdoor unit to heat and cool the whole home.

The 2024 range covers Premium Inverter, Inverter, Slimline and Bulkhead models from 2.5 kW to 24.0 kW, most using R32 refrigerant.

Download brochure (PDF)
Key features

What it offers

  • Whole-home comfort

    The indoor unit is concealed in the ceiling, so it does not compromise the look of your home.

  • Up to 8 zones

    Manage up to 8 zones, switching rooms on or off from the zone controller.

  • Night Quiet mode

    Night Quiet mode reduces outdoor unit noise levels by 4 dBA.

  • R32 refrigerant

    R32 models use a refrigerant with a substantially lower global warming potential than R410A.

  • Australian made

    Premium Inverter, Inverter and Bulkhead indoor units are designed and manufactured in Sydney.

  • Airbase app control

    The optional Airbase adaptor lets you control the system from a smartphone or tablet.
