Overview

About the Emerald Energy 270L all-in-one

The Emerald all-in-one heat pump puts the tank and heat pump in a single unit. Its DC inverter technology is designed to extend component life and keep noise down.

A simple controller lets you set the water temperature, pick a running mode and schedule heating times.

Download controller instructions (PDF)
Key features

What it offers

  • DC inverter technology

    Efficient inverter operation that helps components last longer and reduces noise.

  • Silent mode

    Runs the heat pump at a lower frequency for one quieter heating cycle.

  • Booster mode

    Runs at a higher heating capacity for one cycle when you need hot water sooner.

  • Timer control

    Set up to three timer periods so the system heats when it suits you.

  • Daily legionella control

    Heats at least 45% of the tank to 60ºC every day, whatever the controller settings.
